ABOUT US

Who We Are

Founded in 2014 in Dubai, UAE, our company has grown into a reputable and diversified general trading business serving commercial and industrial clients across the region. We focus on the buying and selling of used vehicles, heavy machinery, and ferrous and non-ferrous metal scrap, along with providing professional junk collection, removal, and disposal services for warehouses, factories, offices, and business facilities.

From humble beginnings, we have developed into a reliable industry partner known for transparency, efficiency, and consistent service quality. Our expertise covers a wide range of sectors, enabling us to meet the trading and disposal needs of businesses of all sizes—whether they are upgrading equipment, clearing space, or disposing of unwanted materials.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted and leading scrap trading and recycling companies in the UAE by delivering reliable, ethical, and environmentally responsible trading solutions.We envision a future where waste materials are transformed into valuable resources through efficient recycling practices, contributing to sustainable development and environmental protection.

expanding our operations, adopting modern processing methods, and maintaining strong relationships with suppliers and clients, we aim to support a cleaner and greener tomorrow.Our vision is driven by quality, transparency, and long-term growth ensuring value for our partners while playing an active role in building a sustainable recycling ecosystem.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver dependable, responsible, and environmentally conscious recycling services that support long-term sustainability across the region. We aim to strengthen the recycling ecosystem by ensuring safe handling, processing, and trading of metals and other recyclable materials.

Through continuous improvement, operational transparency, and strong supplier relationships, we strive to offer consistent quality, prompt service, and reliable trading solutions to all our clients.

Our Values

Integrity

We operate with honesty, fairness, and transparency in every transaction. Our ethical trading practices ensure long-term trust with suppliers and clients.

Sustainability

We prioritize recycling practices that reduce waste, conserve resources, and support a cleaner environment for future generations.

Customer Commitment

We believe in providing high-quality service, timely communication, and complete satisfaction. Our customers remain our highest priority in all operations.
